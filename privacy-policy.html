Pyro Fusion Privacy Policy
Last Updated: November 27, 2025

Pyro Fusion is a rhythm game developed by Denver Cross. i value your privacy.

Data Collection
- High score names (up to 6 letters) and scores are stored locally on your device using localStorage. This data is not shared or transmitted.
- The app includes ads by Adsterra, which may collect anonymous data (e.g., ad impressions, clicks). See Adsterra’s Privacy Policy: https://adsterra.com/privacy-policy/

Data Sharing
No data is shared with third parties, except as described for Adsterra ads.
